                      Help

                      The orb - each small orb on the rings is a feature you have switched on, in its own color. Click the big orb to type to Echo. When you say "night Echo" (or switch Echo off) it goes to sleep and dims.

                      Left arrow - Mission Control. Pick a section; the same arrow takes you back.

                      Right arrow - Live: what's playing, your last request, the phone line, projects, the mic and the PC.

                      Sir Page - Echo phones you when something happens: a caller turned away, a contact on a call, or Desk Guard locking the PC.

                      Two replies at once - Echo can answer you while a call is going, for about 1 GB more graphics memory.

                      Location - Off, PC or Phone: only the device you pick shares its position.

                      Browser control - Echo clicks and types in your browser tab with its own cursor, never your real mouse.
